Strip color check: each button lights the whole strip in one color for 8 seconds.
These sliders only change the real strip, not the screen. Tap White and slide the top three until the strip looks white. Then tap Orange and slide the midtones until it looks orange, not pink (green further left = more orange; blue further right = less pink).
Listening happens on this phone. The speech model (about 40 MB) downloads once. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere; only light colors go to the blanket.

Lully Glimmer is the clean-slate version: it reacts to the reader's voice, magic words and the story's mood, and walks the child to sleep. The original Lully is here. Both use the same blanket pairing code.
